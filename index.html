Hi There!
I'm a PhD Candidate in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at IIIT Delhi (2021–2026), working in the area of Wireless and Ubiquitous sensing for human-centric applications, human activity recognition, and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC). I am working in the WiNSys Lab @ IIIT Delhi supervised by Dr. Mukulika Maity.

Thanks to IIIT Delhi's overseas research fellowship, I got to spent some time (April 2025 to September 2025) at University of Maryland, College Park, USA as visiting research scholar in the Dept. of Computer Science and Engineering. There, I got the opportunity to work closely with Dr. Nirupam Roy where we explored wireless sensing solutions for robust and efficient human activity recognition in complex multi-person environments.

Before my PhD, I completed an M.Tech in Mobile Computing at NIT Hamirpur (Gold Medalist) and a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at PSIT Kanpur, and spent several years as an Assistant Professor.
